# Alex's Mobs (Updated & Ported) — 0.1.0 · First Fabric Beta

The first public beta of **Alex's Mobs** ported to **Minecraft 26.1.2** on **Fabric**. The full
roster is in and playable — this build is for testing and feedback.


## What's included

- **~116 mobs** — the complete Alex's Mobs roster, with their models, textures, and animations.
- **Behaviour** — taming, breeding, mob-specific abilities, attacks, and natural spawning.
- **Items, blocks, and spawn eggs** for every mob.
- Fabric + Fabric API.

## Known issues (beta)

- A few **3D item icons** (the transmutation table item, advancement mob-icons) use flat
  placeholder icons for now — the special 3D item-renderer is still being ported.
- The transmutation table **block** renders as a translucent obsidian cube — this is how the
  original mod renders it, not a bug.
- Some per-mob behaviours are still being verified; please report anything that looks off.

## Feedback

This is a **beta** — bug reports, crashes, and "this mob does X in the original but Y here" notes
are very welcome.

> Forge and NeoForge builds are in progress but not yet ready.

# 1.0.0 — First full Fabric release

The first stable release of **Alex's Mobs Continued** for **Minecraft 26.1.2** on **Fabric**. The
complete Alex's Mobs roster is in, playable, and ported as close to the original as possible.

## Highlights

- **The complete roster — ~116 mobs**, each with its original model, textures, and animations.

  attacks, egg-laying, bucketing, and riding.
- **All the content** — items, blocks, armor, and a spawn egg for every mob.

# 1.0.2 — Bug-fix round

A big pass of fixes from player bug reports: two crashes, a lot of things that rendered wrong, and
several tamed-mob behaviours that simply weren't working. Everything here is on both **Minecraft
26.1.2** and **Minecraft 26.2**.

## Crashes

- **Fixed a crash when feeding a mob** — giving a maned wolf an apple (and various other mob/food
  combinations) could take the game down while it spawned the eating particles.
- **Fixed a crash when putting a carpet on an elephant that already had one.** The carpet did get
  applied — you'd see it after rejoining — but the game crashed on the spot. Raccoons and bald eagles
  had the same problem.

## Tamed mobs

- **Tamed mobs have an owner again.** Taming a crow, gorilla, grizzly bear, elephant, raccoon,
  capuchin monkey or cosmaw showed the hearts but never recorded who tamed it, so the mob kept
  behaving as if it were wild: it wouldn't follow you, wouldn't obey commands, and wouldn't help you
  in a fight.
- **Kangaroo pouch equipment works.** Weapons and armour put in a kangaroo's pouch are now equipped
  and rendered, and the kangaroo fights with them.
- **Restored forced mounting**, plus the two mob-specific command messages that were missing when
  cycling a tamed mob's orders.

## Rendering

- **The Animal Dictionary works.** Previously you could right-click it exactly once — you'd hear the
  page-turn, get the advancement, and then it could never be opened again, even after rejoining. The
  book now opens properly, and its text and mob portraits render correctly.
- **Baby mobs are the right size again.** Around 50 mobs rendered their babies at adult size, or sunk
  into the ground. (Reported for the baby cockroach.)
- **Fixed the armour models** for the Unsettling Kimono, Tarantula Hawk Elytra, Rock Shell
  Chestplate, Novelty Hat, Cave Centipede Leggings, Antler Headdress, Outback Leggings,
  Frontiersman's Cap, Flying Fish Boots and Frostwalker Helmet — the custom-shaped ones rendered
  wrong, and two of the leggings rendered nothing at all.
- **Restored the Rocky Chestplate's rolling animation** — rolling now looks like rolling.
- **The soul vulture has both wings.**
- **The endergrade's saddle shows up** once you saddle it.
- **Cockroaches wear the sombrero again, and dance.**
- **Riders no longer float.** Sitting on an elephant — and on every other rideable mob — put you
  about a third of a block too high above its back.

## Items

- **Music discs play.** Right-clicking a jukebox with an Alex's Mobs disc did nothing at all; the
  Daze and Thime discs are proper jukebox songs now.
- **Fixed two items showing their internal name** (`item.alexsmobs.…`) instead of a proper one.

## Requirements

**Minecraft 26.1.2**

- Fabric Loader 0.18.6+ · Forge 64+ · NeoForge
- Fabric API (Fabric only)
- **CodxLib 1.1.0+** — required, install it alongside this mod

**Minecraft 26.2**

- Fabric Loader 0.19.3+ · Forge 65+ · NeoForge
- Fabric API (Fabric only)
- **CodxLib 1.3.3+** — required, install it alongside this mod

## Fabric

Fabric used to mean two files, for 26.1 and 26.2 only, built from a separate project. All 17 Fabric
builds now come out of the same source as the Forge and NeoForge ones. Fixes land on all three loaders
at once instead of Fabric trailing a version behind.

Nothing was left out to get there: every mob, the multi-part snakes and worms, the custom armour models,
brewing, loot, structure spawns, the falconry glove, the animal dictionary. A handful of small things
work differently on Fabric and they're listed at the bottom.

## Every item was a purple cube on 1.21.4 and newer

Someone reported the falconry glove doesn't appear worn. The glove turned out to be one of 280 items
that were all broken.

Minecraft 1.21.4 changed how an item finds its model and the mod had never been updated for it. On every
build for 1.21.4 and up, which is 6 of the 18 files in the last release, every single item in the mod
rendered as the purple and black missing-model cube. In your hand, in your inventory, on the ground.
Minecraft doesn't treat that as an error, it just draws the cube, so it went unnoticed for three
releases.

Items are correct on every version now. The four with a special in-hand look also have it back: the
falconry glove, skelewag sword, stink ray and vine lasso.

## Fixed from your reports

- Orcas no longer crash the game. If their target died mid-attack the mod kept reading it, which killed
  the tick loop. Servers and singleplayer both.
- Sugar gliders no longer crash when foraging. This one fired every tick, so coming back to the area
  crashed you again straight away.
- Anacondas and void worms don't show a name tag over every body segment any more.
- Centipede heads face where they're going instead of always pointing south.
- Straddleboards move. Since 1.21.2 the board would mount you and play its turning animation but go
  nowhere.
- Crows sit still on your shoulder instead of flapping forever.
- The eagle flight camera doesn't twitch up and down while you steer.
- The Animal Dictionary is readable. Lines were running out of their column into the next one. Chinese,
  Japanese and Korean were worse, since there are no spaces to break at.

Three reports I couldn't reproduce, and I've run out of theories on all of them:

- Attacking the giant squid crashes the game. I need the crash report.
- The blobfish texture looks wrong. The models and the artwork both check out, so I need a screenshot.
- Something teleports around and gets huge at the edge of your vision. Which mob?

Also still open from last release: the rhinoceros that looks yellow with no face. Best guess is a rhino
under a potion effect, which grows a coloured horn, and standing next to one puts your camera inside it.
A screenshot would settle it.

[Discord](https://discord.gg/7jkpWz2Uks) or the issue tracker, either is fine.

## Things that work differently

Each of these is a limit of the Minecraft version or the loader, not a shortcut:

- JEI integration is Forge and NeoForge only, and only up to 1.21.1. JEI doesn't publish a Fabric build
  this mod can use across the rest of the range.
- On 26.1 and 26.2 the mod's villager and wandering trader trades are gone. 26.1 made trades data-driven
  and the old hook no longer exists. Lava Vision also no longer clears the lava you're swimming in,
  though the potion's fog effect still works.
- Snow leopards don't give their bonus drops from their own kills on NeoForge 1.21+ or on Fabric.
  Minecraft 1.21 reworked Looting and removed what this relied on. Looting from your own weapons is
  unaffected.
- On Fabric the ghostly pickaxe renders as its plain model. On Fabric 1.20.1 and 1.20.4 an elephant will
  accept an ender or trapped chest as a howdah. Below Fabric 1.21.4 the jerboa only begs for vanilla
  seeds, not modded ones.
- Eagles can break blocks. That's original Alex's Mobs behaviour, not something I introduced.

## Installing

You need Minecraft, your loader, and [CodxLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/codxlib) for your version and
loader. Fabric users also need Fabric API, which is listed as a dependency on each Fabric file.

You do not need Citadel. The parts of it Alex's Mobs uses are built into the jar. If you already run
Citadel for something else, leave it, the bundled copy is kept separate and they don't clash.

Updating an existing world is fine. Same mod, same content, your mobs and items and progress carry over.

# 2.0.1

One fix, for Minecraft 26.2 only. Every other version is unchanged from 2.0.0 — if you're not on 26.2
there's no reason to hurry.

## Attacking a cachalot, giant squid or laviathan crashed the game

Someone sent the crash report I asked for in the last release, and it turned out not to be a squid
problem at all.

These three mobs are built from separate hitbox pieces. Minecraft 26.2 changed where entities get their
ID number, and those pieces stopped getting one — then reading a missing ID became a crash instead of
returning nothing. So the moment anything asked which piece you hit, the game went down. On Forge it was
worse than that: the crash happened as soon as one of them came into view, before you touched it.

They get an ID again now. That also clears up a quieter one: on 26.2 the game counted every piece of
every one of these mobs as the same piece.

# 2.0.4

A crash fix on NeoForge, a mob you could never tame, and — on Fabric — the repair of something that
has been broken since Fabric support shipped. No new features, nothing that touches your world.

## Fabric: the mod can talk to the server again

**On Fabric, this mod has never sent a single network message.** Anything where your client tells the
server what you did, or the server tells your client what happened, quietly did nothing:

- the bald eagle's falconry — launching it, sending it at an animal, the first-person hooded flight;
- attacking a cachalot whale, giant squid or laviathan **body segment** (the head still worked);
- the transmutation table's buttons;
- ordering a tamed mob around, and the mob-command screens generally.

Some of these were reported as separate bugs. They were one missing piece, now in place on every
Fabric version from 1.20.1 to 26.2. If you play Fabric and had written any of it off as "just doesn't
work in this mod" — try it again.

## Opening a kangaroo's inventory no longer crashes

On NeoForge 1.20.5 and newer, opening a tamed kangaroo's inventory crashed the game. Anything sending
an item across the network hit the same fault, so a kangaroo eating and a few internal messages could
crash too. Fixed.

## Kangaroos no longer destroy their own weapon and armour

Give a kangaroo a sword and it vanished after a single swing — same for a helmet or chestplate the
first time the kangaroo was hit. Rather than taking one point of wear, the item was thrown away
outright. Kangaroo gear now wears down normally and only breaks when its durability runs out.

Affects Minecraft 1.21 and newer, on every loader.

## Komodo dragons can be tamed

The list of foods that tames a komodo dragon shipped **empty**, so nothing worked and the game gave no
hint why. It is rotten flesh, as it always should have been. Every other tameable mob's food list was
checked at the same time; the komodo was the only one affected.

Worth knowing, because it has always worked this way and looks like a bug: you must hold a **full
stack** of rotten flesh, the whole stack is eaten whether or not it works, and it takes a few stacks
on average. Feeding one at a time will never tame it.

## Also fixed

**The bald eagle's falconry works on Fabric.** Beyond the networking fix above, the attack-key press
that starts the falconry loop was listened for in a way only Forge and NeoForge provide. It now
listens the Fabric way, which also restores the tendon whip's crack.

**Items with no picture in the creative menu.** The shattered dimensional carver, the shield of the
deep and a few others showed as empty slots — no picture at all, though hovering still gave the name.
Two different causes with the same look: on Fabric below 1.21.4, eleven items were drawn by a special
renderer this loader never switched on; on 1.21.4 and newer (all loaders) Minecraft removed that kind
of renderer entirely, so those items now carry ordinary models built into the jar. The end pirate
anchor, its winch and the ship wheel are in the second group. Blocks placed in the world were never
affected — this was only how they looked in your inventory.

## Installing

You need Minecraft, your loader, and [CodxLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/codxlib) for your version and
loader. Fabric users also need Fabric API.

You do not need Citadel — the parts of it Alex's Mobs uses are built into the jar.

Updating from any 2.0.x is a drop-in replacement. Your world carries over.
